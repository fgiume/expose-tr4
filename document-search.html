Document Search

Search all 69 episode documents across 18 sanctions cases. Find relevant passages and open the original research document.

Search covers the supplied episode documents, including their coding tables and report extracts. It does not search the complete UN reports. Matches are whole words, ignoring case and accents; common English words are ignored. Repeated passages in a document count as repeated matches.

How ranking works

BM25 scores documents using word frequency, how rare each word is across the collection, and document length. A high score means a better keyword match, not greater factual reliability. “Most word matches” sorts by the total occurrences of your search words. All 69 documents contribute to ranking statistics, even when filters are applied.

Search is lexical: it does not infer synonyms or translate terms. Quotes and punctuation are treated as separators, not phrase-search commands. Dates are episode dates as supplied, not report publication dates. “Ongoing” follows the documents’ 31/12/2025 reference date.
