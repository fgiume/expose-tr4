Background

What is a Panel of Experts?

When the UN Security Council imposes sanctions, it often creates a sanctions committee and appoints a Panel of Experts to assist it. The Panel gathers information, investigates reported violations, and publishes reports with findings and recommendations.

Those reports are the evidence base for EXPOSE. They frequently describe in detail how arms embargoes, commodity bans and financial restrictions are circumvented, and they often note that some Member States are not implementing the measures fully.

Our method

How cases are classified

Collect

Gather the published final and mid-term reports of the Panels for each sanctions regime.

Identify

Read each report and mark every passage that describes an evasion of the sanctions measures.

Classify

Code each case by regime, year and one or more evasion categories.

Publish

Add the case, its source and a short summary to the database.
