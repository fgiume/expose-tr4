Countries

Which countries have faced UN sanctions, and for which of them did the Security Council also create a Panel of Experts? Click a country on the map to see the sanctions period, the episodes within it and the types of sanctions imposed.

How to read the map

Select a country

Click a coloured country, or choose one from the lists below, to see the dates of the sanctions period, the dates of each episode and the types of sanctions.

Red: UN sanctions plus a Panel of Experts (the countries EXPOSE studies). Amber: UN sanctions but no Panel, so outside our study. Grey: no UN sanctions regime. A country with several regimes (for example Libya I and II) shows all of them, and takes the red colour if at least one had a Panel.

About this map. It shows sanctions imposed by the UN Security Council only. Countries such as Russia or Venezuela face sanctions from the EU, the US and others, but not UN sanctions, so they appear in grey. Dates and sanction types come from UN SanctionsApp, which covers regimes since 1991; Southern Rhodesia and South Africa pre-date it. The Al-Qaida / ISIL regime is global, so it appears in the list below the map, not on it. Regimes can change every year, so always check the UN Security Council sanctions pages before citing dates.
Studied by EXPOSE

Sanctions + Panel of Experts

Countries whose sanctions regime was monitored by a Panel or Group of Experts. Their reports are the evidence base of the database. The Al-Qaida / ISIL regime is global and not tied to one country.

Outside our study

Sanctions without a Panel of Experts

The Security Council imposed sanctions here, but no expert panel was created, so there are no comparable reports to code.
